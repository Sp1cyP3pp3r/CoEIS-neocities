Wayfare

Hopscotch

1lv power of the Wayfare
Produce an impossible leap, either up or down, and suffer no damage from the resulting collision.

Quicksilver

2lv power of the Wayfare
Allows an object to move at an incredibly fast speed.

Portal Passage

3lv power of the Wayfare
Creates a temporary opening through any barrier, allowing it to be moved through.

Wind Runner

4lv power of the Wayfare
Allows a target to fly, taking to the sky as if they had wings.

Flicker Flash

5lv power of the Wayfare
Instantly disappear, and reappear somewhere else in the world, or in the Dreaming (though, it is usually unwise to use it to cross between the two).
